# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Credimi is an open community. We want participation here to be a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- Be kind, welcoming, and patient.
- Give and receive feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent; disagree constructively, on the merits.

Behaviour we will not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats in any form.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or development.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other forum we run — and when an individual represents the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project team at <info@forkbomb.eu>. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the team will keep the
reporter's identity confidential to the extent permitted by law.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may face
temporary or permanent removal from the project.
