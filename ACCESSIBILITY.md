# Accessibility Statement

## Our commitment

We are working to make Credimi usable by everyone, including people with
disabilities. Accessibility is a requirement for every change, not an
afterthought.

## What we do

- Semantic HTML and ARIA where needed.
- Keyboard-navigable controls with visible focus states.
- Colour choices that meet contrast requirements.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Known limitations

This is an active project. If you find a barrier, it is likely a bug, not a
design decision — please report it.

## Reporting a barrier

If something is hard or impossible to use, open an issue with the issue
templates, or email <puria@forkbomb.eu>. Describe what you were trying to
do, what happened, and the browser or assistive technology you use.
