## FAQ

### Do I need to run Credimi to use the Extras?

No. Open the hosted tools individually. Each project links to its own source and setup instructions. Use Credimi pipelines when you want to schedule or repeat an end-to-end test.

### Do the Extras certify my implementation?

No. They expose protocol behavior, assess specific artifacts, or link to the relevant requirements. Test with synthetic credentials and test infrastructure, not production personal data or secrets. These results do not replace formal conformance assessment or certification.
